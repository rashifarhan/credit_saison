Where HubSpot's Free Plan Actually Bites

Three limits show up in practice, almost always in this order.

User seats. HubSpot's free CRM allows 2 users. A 5-person sales team hits that wall immediately, not eventually.

Feature gating. Sales automation, custom reporting, and most of the useful email-sequence tooling live behind paid tiers — the free plan covers contact storage and basic pipeline, not much more.

The upgrade nudge never really stops. Even within the free tier, HubSpot surfaces paid features constantly. That's not a bug; it's the growth strategy. It's just worth knowing you're inside one.

The Real Differences Underneath the Table

Bitrix24's free tier is the most seat-generous of the group — up to 12 users — but the trade-off is a genuinely sprawling interface. It bundles CRM with project management, a website builder, and a chat tool, and a small sales team can spend real time just finding the CRM inside everything else.

Zoho CRM's free tier is tight (3 users, no email integration) but the paid tier is competitively priced and well-documented, which matters if you expect to outgrow "free" within a year anyway.

Freshsales' free plan covers the basics cleanly but, like Zoho, drops email integration behind a paywall — exactly the feature most sales teams reach for first.

EzNxT's free tier covers 10 users with email activity logging included, and the CRM shares a login with HR and accounting rather than being a standalone product. Said plainly: EzNxT doesn't have HubSpot's marketing-automation depth (landing pages, workflow builders, ad tracking) — if that's specifically what you need, HubSpot's paid tiers still do more there than any of these alternatives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which free CRM has no contact or user limits at all?
None of them are entirely limit-free — that's how freemium works. EzNxT comes closest for small teams, with no fixed contact cap and 10 free users; Freshsales also has no hard contact ceiling on its free tier but caps at 3 users.

Does switching off HubSpot's free CRM mean losing my contact data?
No. Export contacts and deal history as CSV from HubSpot before migrating, and import into the new CRM. Keep the HubSpot export on file in case you need historical reference later.

Is Bitrix24 good for a small sales team, or is it too much?
Bitrix24 is worth trying if you also want a lightweight project-management tool bundled in. If you want just a CRM, its breadth can be more friction than feature for a small team.

What does EzNxT's CRM not do that HubSpot's paid tiers do?
Marketing automation depth — landing page builders, multi-step ad-driven workflows, advanced attribution reporting. EzNxT is built for sales pipeline, HR, and accounting working together, not for running marketing campaigns.
